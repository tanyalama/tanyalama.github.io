## Redefining colloquia

As a Broader Impacts objective of her [NSF Postdoctoral Research Fellowship](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2010853), Tanya led an initiative to rethink who is invited to departmental colloquia and what they present. The initiative drew on her experience in the student-led [BRIDGE program](https://blogs.umass.edu/bridge/) at UMass. Traditional colloquia rarely recognize the heavy service loads that scholars from historically excluded groups often carry. The approach combines goal-oriented speaker invitations with a lead-by-example practice of including teaching, service, and inclusion work in talks. In Spring 2021 it reshaped Stony Brook's colloquium series: 9 of 13 speakers were from underrepresented groups, and three spoke on teaching and service rather than research, a first for the department.

We maintain an [open-source list of early-career scholars](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eR0Q8R1Dk2iPP__fIKWOqptL-5varoDixBTafnd8RLI/edit?usp=sharing) who self-identify as members of groups underrepresented in academia.
